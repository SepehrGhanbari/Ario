## Contribution ≠ official authority / مشارکت ≠ اختیار رسمی

Anyone may propose issues, code, tests, research, and documentation. Submitting a pull request, running tests, creating a fork, obtaining infrastructure access, or receiving an AI recommendation does **not** by itself authorize an official Ario release, establish Owner identity, transfer private signing authority, or make a claim true.

Official-project changes should have attributable provenance, relevant review and risk-proportionate tests, preserved historical evidence, and a recorded admission decision. Rejection and revision must not silently erase earlier history.

**فارسی:** مشارکت آزاد است، اما پیشنهاد، دسترسی فنی یا قبولی تست، مجوز انتشار رسمی و اختیار مالک ایجاد نمی‌کند. منشأ، شواهد، تصمیم و سابقهٔ اصلاحات باید قابل ممیزی بمانند.

Keys, passwords, private vaults, recovery, real or synthetic signing and trust activation require their own explicit Owner approval and security gates. Neither this policy, repository access, a contributor, an automated agent, nor a CI pass grants such authority.

## Continuity and succession / تداوم و جانشینی

Ario aims to be maintainable beyond the founder's constant presence, without automatically passing official Owner authority to someone who holds code, hosting access, or a fork. **No official succession/delegation mechanism has yet been adopted.** Future delegation must be explicitly authorized, versioned, auditable, limited in scope, and clear about key loss, revocation, disputes and historical preservation.

**فارسی:** ادامهٔ توسعه باید ممکن باشد، اما دسترسی به سورس یا سرور، جانشینی و حق تصاحب هویت رسمی آریو نیست. جانشینی هنوز تعیین و تصویب نشده است.
