# R22.26 — challenge-bound quorum gossip and joint epoch rotation

RESEARCH_ONLY / DRAFT / HOLD / NO_REAL_KEYS / NO_TRUST_ACTIVATION / NO_PRODUCTION / NO_AUTOMATIC_MERGE.

Parent #396, 1634bbc3d5c704044b51da2ce8f2a57efd65ec0e. Isolated standard-library Go model; no changes to R22.22–25 or scheduled readbacks. Deterministic publicly known test-only Ed25519 seeds. No external writes or provisioned services.

Read policy pins scope, ordered membership, key and claimed administrative-domain identifiers, epoch and threshold. Every reply binds the same policy hash, nonce, scope, epoch and ordered receipt-digest history. All supplied replies are checked; a signed disagreement forces HOLD, not majority selection. A returning client's exact retained prefix must match even if a candidate has a higher generation. Insufficient quorum may expose an explicitly unadmitted observation, never a new floor. Results are REVIEWABLE_ONLY or HOLD; no admission/production semantics.

Counterexamples: two compromised members of 2/3 can sign fresh challenges over a stale history while the honest newer witness is suppressed; one equivocator can form two conflicting 2/3 read quorums with two distinct honest signers, each signing only one branch. For intersection of any two size-q sets among n, minimum shared members is max(0,2q-n). To guarantee at least one honest intersection member with at most f Byzantine identities, require 2q-n>f. This is a necessary intersection condition under a durable honest non-equivocation assumption, not a consensus implementation. 3/3 fails closed if one member is absent; 3/4 has honest intersection for f<=1, but any returned disagreement triggers HOLD, so Byzantine denial-of-service remains possible. All compromised witnesses can still mislead a cold reader. A single newer surviving witness can provide an authenticated observation for investigation, not a quorum admission.

Rotation requires unanimous old and new membership approvals of the exact old/new policy hashes, epochs, independently retained prior floor and nonce; no automatic removal of an unreachable old member. This intentionally prioritizes safety over recovery availability. Signatures alone do not prove independent admins, truthful log data, live freshness, old-key erasure, durable non-equivocation, or atomic persistence of the new policy across clients. Claimed domain identifiers are only syntactic uniqueness checks. No transport, live proof verifier, actual witness service, receipt preimage recovery, keeper integration, or persistent rotation state is implemented. Receipt digest history in this model is a synthetic ordered hash chain, not a verified R22.25 receipt chain. Read challenge generation/retention belongs to the caller; fixture nonce is deterministic.
