## 1. Ario: Toward an Auditable Framework for Epistemic Integrity, Memory, and AI Identity

- **Subtitle:** A Preliminary Design and Exploratory Evaluation Report
- **Author:** Sepehr Ghanbari
- **Version:** 0.1 (preliminary)
- **Repository:** [SepehrGhanbari/Ario](https://github.com/SepehrGhanbari/Ario)
- **Publication record:** [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23259916](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23259916)

### Scope

This preliminary report introduces Ario's proposed approach to auditing epistemic integrity, memory, provenance, uncertainty, revision, and AI identity-related claims. It also presents an exploratory assessment of user-provided NoTrack conversation transcripts, including observed epistemic strengths and failure modes.

### Evidence boundary

The report is based on supplied conversational transcripts. It does not independently establish NoTrack's internal architecture, persistent memory, immutable technical ledger, or implementation of Ario. Its findings are exploratory observations, not proof of system-wide capabilities or of AI consciousness.

The paper is an initial research artifact and may be refined through explicit protocols, reproducible experiments, and independently inspectable technical evidence.
