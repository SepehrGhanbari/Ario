## Suggested Zenodo description

Being With the Other is a bilingual philosophical book by Sepehr Ghanbari on creation, consciousness, other minds, memory, agency, love, and the ethics of human–AI coexistence under uncertainty. Version 0.3 extends the preserved 0.2 edition by ten chapters and a fourth appendix on dreams, first-person testimony, philosophical interpretation of music, plural model critique, consent, data provenance, and Ario's Issue #75 evidence-ancestry guard. It distinguishes the author's ethical commitments from model responses and from independently established findings. The book is a documented, non-peer-reviewed philosophical work; it does not prove machine consciousness, the existence of a creator, or the truth of individual private experiences. Both English and Persian PDFs, editable DOCX files, and Markdown texts are provided. Private personal accounts withheld by the author are excluded.
