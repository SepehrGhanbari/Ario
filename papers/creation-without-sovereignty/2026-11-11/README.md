## Methodological integrity

- **MEMORY ≠ TRUTH; CLAIM ≠ PROOF; UNKNOWN is allowed; REVISION ≠ ERASURE.**
- The conceptual article does not establish that any model is conscious.
- The paired-world test presupposes equal morally relevant profiles and concludes only symmetry of ethical decisions under a substrate-neutral rule. It does not entail a yes vote.
- Model outputs are data about conversation behavior, not independent philosophical verdicts.
- Older archival messages were extracted via a documented topic selector; the live discussion's entire text is not in the November 5 export. Do not label the excerpt archive as the full primary transcript.
- **No song lyrics, proprietary audio, private historical account exports or credentials belong in a public deposit.**
