# Source, privacy, and copyright policy for this release

This package separates the author's original scholarly arguments and dialogue-based commentary from (a) copyrighted songs and sound recordings, (b) unpublished full private conversation archives, and (c) unverifiable assertions concerning model experience or the real world.

## Included in the public-release package

- Original research manuscript in English and Persian, with references and explicit limitation labels.
- Detailed but **not verbatim-complete** discussion process record, plus a selection of exact user-authored anchor messages.
- A case-linked claim–evidence ledger, proposed experimental protocol, a source inventory and file SHA-256 manifest.
- Links to account-specific original ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude chats. These are not guaranteed to be externally viewable.

## Explicitly excluded from public GitHub/Zenodo distribution

- The 247 MB `conversations(2).json` account export, which includes unrelated private conversations. A SHA-256 is preserved for local verification.
- Raw third-party song lyrics, music files and cover images. The author may separately seek permission where needed; references to works do not transfer copyright.
- Secrets, private credentials, contact details or local operational paths from unrelated Ario conversations.
- A falsely labeled “full verbatim 11 November transcript.” The provided export predates this exchange; the true live transcript remains in the original chat sources.

## Rights and proposed license

Subject to the human author's review, the **new original manuscript prose and analysis** may be deposited under CC BY 4.0. That license does not license any third-party music, proprietary platform interfaces, private conversations belonging to others, or any underlying files whose rights have not been confirmed. The source-derived supplementary material may warrant a more restrictive or separate license. Zenodo requires the owner to make and verify that choice before publication.

## Epistemic limitations

- No direct experiment on welfare, consciousness or evolved civilizations was performed.
- Some model-to-model correspondence was available through a browser and described in a prior handoff note, but the exact complete exchange was not re-exported as an authenticated machine-readable transcript in this artifact session.
- The extracted archive is a **filtered corpus** from 9 selected earlier conversations, not 75 complete conversations and not the new November discussion. The extraction records how items were selected and what was redacted.
- Any sentence reporting “the models changed their minds” should be understood as **outputs changing over the transcript**, not verified private belief revision.
