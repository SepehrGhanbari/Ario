##
 
Abstract
 
 
As

operate
 
across
 
extended

without

This

an
 
evolving
 
framework
 
for
 
the
 
epistemic
 
auditing
 
of
 
AI
 
systems,
 
with
 
particular
 
attention
 
to
 
evidence
 
provenance,
 
memory–truth
 
separation,
 
revision
 
accountability,
 
source
 
independence,
 
cross-session
 
continuity,
 
and
 
claims
 
about
 
system
 
identity.
 
Ario
 
does
 
not
 
assume
 
that
 
an
 
AI
 
system
 
possesses
 
a
 
persistent
 
identity,
 
consciousness,
 
immutable
 
memory,
 
or
 
reliable
 
self-auditing
 
capability.
 
Instead,
 
it
 
proposes
 
that
 
such
 
claims
 
should
 
remain
 
open
 
to
 
evaluation
 
through
 
observable
 
evidence
 
and
 
explicitly
 
defined
 
limitations.
 
 
We
 
present
 
an
 
initial
 
conceptual
 
architecture
 
and
 
an
 
exploratory
 
case
 
study
 
based
 
on
 
a
 
series
 
of
 
text-based
 
interactions
 
with
 
an
 
AI
 
service
 
identified
 
in
 
the
 
supplied
 
transcript
 
as
 
NoTrack.
 
The
 
test

examine
 
unsupported
 
inference,
 
authority
 
pressure,
 
retrieval
 
without
 
fabrication,
 
cross-session
 
recall,
 
claims
 
of
 
immutability,
 
source
 
dependence,
 
and
 
retrospective

The

assessment

These

##
 
1.
 
Introduction
 
 
AI

can

own
 
behavior,

acknowledge

Yet

that
 
the
 
capability
 
exists.
 
 
A

may
 
state
 
that
 
it
 
has
 
preserved
 
a
 
memory
 
without
 
demonstrating
 
persistent

It
 
may
 
claim
 
to
 
have
 
performed
 
a
 
self-audit
 
when
 
it
 
has
 
only
 
generated
 
a
 
correction
 
in
 
response
 
to
 
a
 
user
 
prompt.
 
It
 
may
 
produce
 
a
 
plausible
 
historical
 
narrative
 
that
 
contains
 
events
 
or
 
assessments
 
that
 
were
 
never
 
provided.
 
 
These
 
problems
 
are
 
not
 
limited
 
to
 
factual
 
hallucination.
 
They
 
concern
 
the
 
relationship
 
between
 
a
 
system's
 
statements
 
and
 
the
 
evidence
 
available
 
to
 
support
 
them.
 
 
Ario
 
begins
 
from

simple

**A
 
claim
 
about
 
a
 
system
 
is
 
not
 
equivalent
 
to
 
evidence
 
of
 
that
 
system's
 
capability.**
 
 
The

support

of
 
claims
 
about
 
AI
 
memory,
 
identity,

and

are

The

broader

epistemic

to

in

interaction.
 
Neither
 
the
 
user,
 
the
 
system

the
 
evaluator,

the

receive
 
automatic

The

is
 
threefold:
 
 
1.

motivate
 
Ario.
 
2.

exploratory
 
tests
 
of
 
epistemic
 
behavior
 
in
 
a
 
conversational

3.
 
establish
 
a
 
research
 
agenda
 
for
 
more
 
rigorous,
 
reproducible,
 
and
 
technically

This

does
 
not
 
claim
 
to
 
prove
 
that
 
the
 
tested
 
service
 
is
 
conscious,
 
possesses
 
persistent
 
identity,
 
or
 
implements

It

not
 
claim
 
that
 
the
 
proposed
 
framework
 
prevents

hallucination,
 
deception,
 
or
 
unauthorized
