## Research question

Does the graph-based Ario TB-003 evaluator distinguish supplied conditional separation from *real independently authenticated provenance*, especially when control or source edges are omitted while the graph is falsely declared complete?

**Existing evidence:** The 16/16 versus 4/16 historical test is a co-designed synthetic comparison; the W1-A run produced 80 deterministic versions of just eight logical families and observed 30 mismatches in three hidden-link families. Neither is blinded external-world validation. A conditional positive under falsely complete laboratory premises is **not by itself a bug in TB-003**.

## Evidence-aware synthesis (original digest)

1. **Graph topology is not the whole world.** Internal consistency and even authenticated logs for a *declared capture scope* cannot establish that all relevant physical/controller/source links were captured.
2. **Copy detection ≠ custody authentication.** Truth-discovery models infer likely copying from value correlations. Ario evaluates declared graph edges. A source-sharing inference cannot attest separate administrators or actual custody.
3. **Three-valued UNKNOWN is legitimate.** Insufficient observations must not be forced into dependent or independent. Rates must expose abstention and coverage.
4. **Identity diversity ≠ controller diversity.** Multiple signers, devices, digital identities or witnesses can remain under one shared root.
5. **Blinded design requires externally held oracles.** Independent challenge authors and two labelers should freeze labels before evaluator output; masked input, prediction commitment and private gold must be separated by custody and access boundary. Source hashes establish only exact bytes, not independent authorship or chronology.
6. **Twelve candidate stressor prompts:** omitted administrative events; transitive source aliases; compromised signing root; time-order ambiguity; signed false completeness; downstream provenance laundering; collusive split graphs; long-running service dependence; Sybil witness count; scope-misaligned envelope/payload hashes; omitted derivation steps; partition/revocation rollback. These are prompts to *new authors*, not twelve independent evaluated worlds or known answers.
7. **Metric separation:** visible-contract consistency, latent-source/input fidelity, downstream false promotions; report each axis, oracle disagreements, unknowns, scope exclusions, and negative controls separately.
8. **Non-overclaim rule:** research-only integrity/status observations cannot confer memory admission, genuine historical attestation, signer approval, trust activation or real-world certainty.
