What this page deliberately does not claim

No real participant count, validated GPU/CPU/phone earnings, independent historical attestation, permanent signing key status, genesis activation or trust approval is inferred from these public traces.

Traffic and download counters are imperfect indicators of attention, not evidence of an individual using Ario. Any future opt-in service trial requires explicit consent and separate evidence of performance, demand and device safety.
