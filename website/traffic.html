What these numbers do and do not mean

Clones are retrieval events, not people or verified software users. Page views do not identify individuals. Automation may contribute to traffic, but this aggregate does not attribute identity, origin, or intent.

Numbers are transcribed from a private, owner-maintained capture of GitHub's Traffic API. Original raw snapshots remain private. No private referrer or popular-path lists, review comments, tokens, or personal information are published.

Historical evidence outside this captured period is incomplete and is not added here. No claim of independent historical attestation, an all-time audience count, adoption, or causal effect of a release is made.
